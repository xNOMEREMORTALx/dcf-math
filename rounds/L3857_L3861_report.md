## L3857: combined test (4 of 4)
Each family's share minus the other-gene share was turned into a genome-shift z in every cell (1,000 shifts, seed 3857), and the ten z values were combined (Stouffer).

| # | family | prediction | combined Z | one-sided p | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | K⁺ channels (96) | Z > 1.645 | **+4.50** | 3 × 10⁻⁶ | **PASS** |
| S2 | NMDA receptors (7) | Z > 1.645 | **+4.24** | 1 × 10⁻⁵ | **PASS** |
| S3 | Ca²⁺ channel pores (10) | Z < −1.645 | **−3.77** | 8 × 10⁻⁵ | **PASS** |
| S4 | all 139 genes | Z > 3.09 | **+5.46** | 2 × 10⁻⁸ | **PASS** |

**Caution:** Stouffer's method assumes the ten cells are independent. They are not: the genes are the same, and many CTCF sites are shared between cell types. So these Z values **overstate** the evidence. The defensible reading is that the direction is consistent in nearly every cell. No single cell reaches z = 2 for Ca²⁺ pores, but all ten point the same way.

## L3861: matching for length *and* local gene density (4 of 4)
Local density is the number of genes within ±1 Mb. Controls are the 20 nearest non-channel genes in length and density, and the medians match: K⁺ density 14 vs 14; NMDA 54 vs 54; **Ca²⁺ pores 36 vs 36**.

| # | prediction (≥ 7 of 10 cells) | result |

| G1 | K⁺ channels above matched controls | **PASS:** 10 of 10 (Δ +0.03 to +0.14) |

| G4 | sparsest-tenth genes below the ordinary share | **PASS:** 10 of 10 |

**Reading:**
- **Gene-poor regions do have fewer anchored genes** (G4).
- **Calcium-pore genes sit in moderately gene-rich neighbourhoods** (median 36 genes per 2 Mb, against 14 for K⁺ channels), so they are not in deserts.
- **After matching, their dip grows:** Δ −0.05 to −0.19.
- **As fixed in advance, G2 passing means the calcium dip is not a gene-desert effect.**

## Summary
- **Across ten human cell types, combined, potassium-channel and NMDA-receptor genes sit at CTCF loop anchors more often than other genes,** and calcium-channel pore genes less often.
- **This survives matching for gene length and for local gene density.**
- **The combined statistics are very large but overstated,** because the cells are not independent. The consistency of direction is the robust claim.
- **Manuscript 3 now has Figure 2,** the ten-cell plot.

## Potential leads for future research
- **[Dependence-aware test]** Resample genes, not anchors: draw random gene sets matched for length and density, compute the ten-cell mean contrast, and compare. This respects the shared-gene dependence.
- **[Out-of-sample]** Fix the statistic now and test it on cell types not yet used (ENCODE T cells, other Ruan-lab sets).
- **[What is special about CACNA1?]** All 10 pore genes are long and sit in moderately dense regions, yet avoid anchors. Look at whether their TSSs fall inside large CTCF-bounded domains rather than at boundaries.
