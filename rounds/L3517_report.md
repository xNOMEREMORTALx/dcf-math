## Result
- **Only 4 proteins are ≥ 1,400 aa, and none has a voltage sensor.**
- **There is no four-domain sodium/calcium-type channel in the file,** so the ring letter cannot be read.
- **As pre-stated, the exploratory question is unanswered, not failed.** It needs the full *Hofstenia* genome's gene set, which is not in UniProt.

## Descriptive: the iron finder in an acoel worm
**What it found:** the tool's only calls were three **iron hydroxylases** (`[CVI]-H-E-[LI]-[LM]-G-H`).

**What UniProt names them:** *Hofstenia* **tryptophan hydroxylase 1, 2 and 3**, the serotonin-making enzymes.

**What that adds:** the iron-site core now matches correctly from humans to the panther worm. That is a seventh lineage, after vertebrates, the fly, the nematode, the placozoan and others. It is not a pre-registered test, so it doesn't count in the record.
