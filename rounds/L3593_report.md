**Reading:**
- **Voltage-gated potassium channels of the Shab and EAG families exist in animals' single-celled relatives and in sponges,** before any nervous system.
- ***Trichoplax*, which has no neurons, already carries Shaker, Shab, Shaw and EAG-type channels, plus a leak channel.** That is most of the fast "reset" toolkit, in place before neurons evolved.
- **Leak (K2P) channels are present in all three.**
- **Taken with L3501 (pumps present even in the choanoflagellate):** both sides of the nerve battery existed before nerves. The chargers (pumps) and much of the discharge machinery (K channels, Ca channels, leak channels) were there already. What arrived later were the DEKA sodium channel (in bilaterians) and lineage-specific expansions (the comb jelly's Shaker pile-up).

## Summary
- **Before nerves existed, the potassium channels did:**
  - a single-celled choanoflagellate has a Shab-type channel;
  - a sponge has an EAG-type channel;
  - the neuron-less placozoan has Shaker, Shab, Shaw and EAG types.
- **The nerve battery's parts came first:** pumps, potassium and calcium channels, leak channels. Nerves then put them to work, adding the DEKA sodium channel later in bilaterians.
- **Figure 6 shows the comb jelly's Shaker pile-up against the spread in other lineages.**
