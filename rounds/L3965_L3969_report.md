## What it means
**The result is split, which the fixed interpretation did not cover (it covered both pass or both fail), so neither reading is applied.** Descriptively:
- **In tissue and neuron Hi-C, loops from pore K⁺ genes reach a gene start at their far end half as often as loops from matched genes.** That is the strongest new result of this session (p = 0.0001).
- **Their partners are mostly non-promoter sites,** which fits a structural (boundary/insulator) loop rather than a promoter–promoter or promoter–enhancer contact.
- **In the cell-line CTCF ChIA-PET maps the direction is the same but weaker.** That is expected, because those maps contain only CTCF loops, which are mostly structural anyway.

**A coherent picture is emerging (to be tested, not concluded):**
- **Pore-forming K⁺ genes sit at loop anchors more often than similar genes** (the main result).
- **Their loops are of normal length** (L3965).
- **The far end is usually not another gene** (L3969).
- **In H1/HFFc6 and T cells, the loops that do carry other genes avoid them** (L3905, L3953).

**Read together:** these genes look like they sit at CTCF boundary points, kept apart from regulatory hubs.
