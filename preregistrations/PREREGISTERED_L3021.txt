PRE-REGISTRATION L3021 L2936f (which primes divide a grid side). Gary NOMEREMORTAL Mason, DCF, 6 Nov 2026. Frozen before the prime survey.
m_k = (a^(2k+1) - a^-(2k+1))/(2 sqrt2), a = sqrt3 + sqrt2. With w = a^2 = 5 + 2 sqrt6 (norm 1): p | m_k <=> w^(2k+1) = 1 mod p.
So a prime p >= 5 divides some grid side exactly when the multiplicative order of w mod p is ODD (order divides p - (6/p)).
G1 Criterion "order of w odd" agrees with brute-force simulation of m_k mod p for every prime 5 <= p < 3000.
G2 Density of primes 5 <= p < 10^5 dividing some grid side lies in [0.30, 0.37] (guess 1/3, as for odd-order density of a generic element).
G3 Split by (6/p): the density is NOT the same for (6/p) = +1 and (6/p) = -1 (difference > 0.03).
