P1  The share of digit 5 among the first 10^6 decimals of pi, of phi and of sqrt 5 is each within 3 sd of 1/10 (sd = 0.0003).
P2  Positions where pi and phi BOTH carry a 5: count within the 99% Poisson interval around N/100 = 10,000.
P3  pi and phi carry the same digit at a share within 3 sd of 1/10 (two unrelated normal-looking numbers: lambda = 1/b).
P4  (theorem check) Golden-angle (Vogel) points: theta_n = n/phi^2 turns. The n <= 10^6 at which theta_n mod 1 comes closer to the
    0-direction (the circle's starting radius) than at any earlier n are exactly the Fibonacci numbers (from 2 on).
P5  For the triangle directions (1/3 turn) and the pentagon directions (1/5, 2/5 turn), the number of such "record approaches" up to 10^6
    lies within +-50% of the count for the 0-direction (record approaches come at the same logarithmic rate, about log_phi N).
