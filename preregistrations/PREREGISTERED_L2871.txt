PART 1 (L2869: does the free mod-63 check catch deletions and insertions?)
Strands: full base-4 periods of 1/p, primes 101 < p < 5000 with 3 | T (as in L2866), 20 random errors each, seed 2871.
Analytic expectation (derived before simulating): if letter d is deleted with suffix value B after it, the shortened number
is = 4^{-1}(3B - d*4^k) mod 63, so it can pass only if d is A (0) or T (3), and then with chance about 1/21.
E1  pure deletion (strand becomes T-1 long): every deletion of a C or G is detected (100%).
E2  pure deletion, all letters: detection between 96.0% and 99.0% (expected about 1 - 1/42 = 97.6%).
E3  fixed-length read window (deletion pulls the next letter of the following period into the window): detection 97.5%-99.2% (1 - 1/63 = 98.4%).
E4  fixed-length window with one insertion (last letter pushed out): detection 97.5%-99.2%.

PART 2 (L2867: does the codon-phase statistic predict gene-finder accuracy?)  NOT blind: both quantities exist from earlier rounds,

Hodgkinia, M. florum, M. mycoides, Ureaplasma, M. pneumoniae, M. genitalium; table 11 otherwise). S = median codon-phase statistic
over annotated genes >= 300 nt (L2866 definition, recomputed here for all 29).
G1  Spearman rho(S, "predictions correct") > 0.3.
G2  Spearman rho(S, "genes found") > 0.3.
G3  In the regression accuracy ~ GC + log S, the log S coefficient is positive.
