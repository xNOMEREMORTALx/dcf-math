 echinoderms, hemichordates; Domain III EEEE/EEKE in arthropods such as myriapods and chelicerates; nematodes, insects, chordates lack the
 alternative pairs; vertebrates EEKE only. Seen before freezing: only the three B. calyciflorus entries (L3649: EKEE- and EEEE-type words).
Method: script docstring. Ring read as 4 letters DI..DIV. UniProt usually lists one isoform per gene, so absence of a variant is weak evidence.
