Theorem L' (derived now): if y = r s^-1 x mod p with gcd(r, s) = 1, then y/p = {(r x/p + k)/s} with k = -r x p^-1 mod s. Counting over
q = leading digit, k < s and t < r gives the exact rational
  lambda_b(r/s) = #{(q, k, t): 0<=q<b, 0<=k<s, 0<=t<r, floor((r q + b k + t)/s) = q (mod b)} / (b r s),
which is the long-run share of positions where x/p and (r/s)x/p carry the same base-b digit (exact up to O(brs)/p when x runs over
all residues, as for a full-reptend prime). Symmetry: lambda(r/s) = lambda(s/r). s = 1 gives Theorem Lambda.
